Weekly CBT Centre

Your weekly examination and performance-monitoring area.

How the CBT works

Weekly CBTs are designed to provide regular JAMB-style practice. Students complete the test seriously, while Prime Star uses the results to monitor performance and identify weak areas.

Performance Reporting

Results are recorded against the student's Prime Star Student ID. Public rankings can be shared using Student IDs rather than students' names, while individual performance records remain private.
